Our Foundation

The Vision of
Logan Westlake

Founded by Logan Westlake, the foundation was born out of a refusal to accept the status quo. Driven by a deep-seated frustration with the neglect of our local thoroughfares, Logan took action.

The Logan Westlake Foundation stands as a symbol of community accountability. We don't just wait for change; we drive it.

Our focus is on more than just "picking up trash" — we are reclaiming the natural beauty of Hernando County for every resident and visitor.

What We Stand For

Our Values

Community Accountability

We don't just wait for change; we drive it. Clean roads are a shared responsibility, and we choose to show up.

Structured & Legal

Our partnership with the Hernando County Adopt-A-Road program keeps every cleanup organized, safe, and above board.

Natural Beauty

More than picking up trash — we're reclaiming Hernando County's roads, lakes, and green spaces for everyone.

How A Cleanup
Day Works

01

Check In

Meet the team at the project site and attend a brief safety orientation.

02

Gear Up

Grab a high-visibility vest, industrial gloves, and a trash picker — all provided.

03

Clean

Work the corridor or shoreline in teams, bagging litter and debris as we go.

04

Wrap Up

Bags are collected for disposal and the site is left better than we found it.

    Questions

    Volunteer FAQ

    Do I need any experience?

    No. Every cleanup starts with a brief safety orientation, and our team will show you everything you need to know.

    What do I need to bring?

    We supply high-visibility vests, industrial gloves, and trash pickers. Wear closed-toe shoes and clothes you don't mind getting dirty, and bring water and sun protection.

    Can I earn community service hours?

    Yes — check the "community service hours" box on the sign-up form and we'll follow up about what you need.

    Can my school, club, or group volunteer together?

    Mention your group and its size in the message field and we'll get back to you.

    When are cleanups held?

    Check the Calendar for upcoming cleanup dates. Meeting points and details are emailed to registered volunteers ahead of every cleanup.
